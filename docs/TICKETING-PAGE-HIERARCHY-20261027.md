# Ticketing page hierarchy, 27 October 2026

## Superseding owner direction

The owner rejected the oversized working queue and the specialist “Pressure drops during service” hero caption, then approved rebuilding after a whole-page review. This supersedes the photograph-led hero and large eight-ticket chapter in TICKETING-WORKING-QUEUE-20261027.md. Successful technical checks on that previous version were not visual acceptance.

The mistake was interpreting more substance as more interface. The new page answers four distinct buyer questions: what needs attention and who has it; what context stays with an issue; whether the process fits different requests; and how the business gets started. Preserve the H1, hero proposition, final CTA, root navigation/footer, Platform architecture, existing leaf route/indexing and /booking sales paths. GitHub owns source changes. Lovable remains read only, with no builder prompt or separate Publish action.

## Critical lenses

These are separate assessments by the implementation agent, not independent reviewers, owner approval or measured conversion findings. No numeric scores are assigned.

| Lens                   | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UI/UX                  | The opening communicates queue, ownership and progress without asking the buyer to operate a demo. Phone rows retain the customer, assigned people and priority. Native links/disclosures work.                |
| Brand and creative     | Warm white, meaningful shallow stage colours, one restrained ink chapter, actual logo and established portraits retain Zapla identity. Photography has a supporting human role.                                |
| Product marketing      | The main distinction is the connected request, customer and related work. Fields/tags are supporting mechanisms rather than separate oversized benefits.                                                       |
| Conversion             | The page moves from recognition to connected value, fit, adoption and the retained call. Setup scope and commercial terms remain available before the closing CTA.                                             |
| SME owner/frontline    | Everyday request names broaden recognition. Shared responsibility and a named manual resolution make the work intelligible without knowing technical equipment vocabulary.                                     |
| Buyer psychology       | The page shows who handles an issue and what happened without invented urgency, scale, response promises or an implied autonomous repair. Existing tools are addressed without assuming wholesale replacement. |
| Copy and awareness     | Preserve the outcome-led H1; give the buyer a concrete mechanism, differentiation, fit and next step. Remove the unexplained pressure phrase and repeated service detail.                                      |
| Motion                 | No playback, timers, hidden content or moving decoration. The complete story works as static content.                                                                                                          |
| Technical and solution | Scope stays within documented boards/stages/people/contacts/links/custom fields. No backend calls, fake download/send controls or new package are introduced.                                                  |
| AI-slop critic         | No decorative counters, icon tiles, arbitrary accent bars, fake testimonials, placeholder addresses or cards added merely to occupy space. Actual ticket cards have a clear role.                              |
