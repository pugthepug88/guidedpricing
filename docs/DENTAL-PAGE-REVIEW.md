# Dental page build and review

Canonical route: `/industries/dental`. GitHub source: `pugthepug88/guidedpricing`. Lovable was not edited. The page retains the site's noindex and sitemap-excluded preview policy. Solutions navigation links to Dental Practices on desktop and mobile. Shared nav geometry, logo and root-owned footer are preserved.

## Recommendation and commercial boundary

Dental merits a standalone page for independent general/family and multi-dentist practices with a demonstrable communication gap. Small groups can use this story, subject to scoped access and data requirements. Hospital dentistry, corporate rollouts, referral-only specialist operations and specialist treatment funnels are not the focus. Cosmetic and implant enquiries may fit an agreed administrative workflow, but this page does not sell treatment acceptance or clinical outcomes.

Organising concept: follow-through between visits. The story is new enquiry → agreed check-in → practice-approved return invitation. Clinical dental software stays authoritative for treatment, clinical records, bookings and recall dates. Zapla must earn its place by solving an execution gap, rather than suggesting incumbent PMSs lack reminders, recall or two-way communications.

Alternatives considered: empty chair capacity is commercially clear but risks overstating integration and directly attributing bookings; patient leakage can sound transactional and pressures the treatment decision; reception memory is recognisable but narrows the platform to inbox/admin. Between visits best combines patient agency, team usefulness and the site's existing follow-through promise. The rejected clinical stock image was inspected. It emphasised treatment and dated blue medical imagery. The new reception image supports a busy receptionist while the product layer handles an enquiry.

Commercial priority: first enquiry response, then agreed administrative follow-up, then eligible reactivation. Routine recall/reminders are not a displacement pitch. Cancellation filling and PMS diary sync are not claimed. Unlimited users supports team participation below the workflow proof, not as the dental pain headline.

## Page architecture and buyer awareness

The hero gives problem-aware visitors a recognisable reception setting and the explicit existing-software boundary. The open relationship section helps less-aware owners see where conversations stall. Three different patient conversations demonstrate response, human questions and campaign eligibility. The dark responsibility section addresses solution-aware duplication and integration objections. Guided Launch addresses product-aware implementation, privacy and staff adoption. Pricing differentiation, practical FAQs and the concrete sales-call agenda support most-aware visitors without a free-trial distraction.

Patient examples contain administrative information only, not treatment prices, symptoms, diagnoses or medical claims. They are illustrations, not live product screenshots, testimonials or observed clinical records. Screen-reader scene labels say so. Booking claims are conditional on a verified connection. Callback requests are shown as requests, not completed appointments.

Functional job: make the next administrative action visible and give reception the conversation. Emotional job: reduce the burden of remembering whom to chase. Social job: give patients a warm, consistent practice experience. Risk-reduction job: preserve embedded clinical software and clinician judgment, verify the actual data path before purchase or import. These jobs are positioning hypotheses, not measured findings from customer interviews.

## Motion and visual QA

The built-in image generation tool created an editorial reception scene with cream, oak and sage, a receptionist occupied with a patient, and no clinical action, text or baked-in UI. Project asset: `public/concept/industries/dental-reception.webp` (128 KB). Prompt intent: candid modern independent Australian dental reception, natural daylight, profile interaction, unattended desk telephone, subtle dental room in background, no posed smiles or medical graphics, lower foreground space for a message overlay. Original generation retained outside the repository.

Established local Inter Tight and Manrope fonts, approved portrait sprite, original Zapla petal and palette reused. Open recognition section, asymmetric photo hero, dialogue scenes, dark responsibility section, unboxed launch list, pricing statement and accordion FAQ produce the whole-page rhythm. No tooth motifs, stock treatment imagery, synthetic charts, clinical testimonials or decorative looping UI.

Dialogues activate near the viewport, reveal after 350ms and 550ms, pause their timers offscreen and hold the final state. Geometry is reserved during opacity reveals. Reduced-motion preference completes the sequence in an effect after hydration, preventing the server/client mismatch found and fixed during QA. Repeating movement is absent, so no pause control is needed for the dental scenes.

Browser inspection: full-page desktop and mobile renders; detailed desktop hero, enquiry and follow-up; homepage, Customer Marketing and mechanics comparison renders. Widths 320, 390, 768 and 1440 had no horizontal overflow. Hero asset loaded. Desktop and mobile dental navigation links found. Native FAQ expands. Reduced-motion scenes all reach state 2 with no dental page errors. Normal-motion scenes remain at state 0 offscreen, progress to 2 when visible, and hold 2. Production build, TypeScript and scoped route ESLint pass. Generated route tree is regenerated by the router, not hand edited.

## Red-team verdict

A practice with effective PMS communication and no demonstrable gap may not need Zapla. Lack of live integration can make some campaigns uneconomic because of maintenance and suppression work. The page states these boundaries rather than inventing superiority. If a proposed setup cannot keep current bookings and preferences accurate, do not run that campaign. If voice cannot safely hand off to a person, do not enable that dental call flow.

Conversion and CFO cannot honestly be rated at least 9 on the current evidence. The next improvement requires a real dental pilot documenting the current gap, configured flow, staff effort, update process and observed result. Repeated copy/design iterations cannot supply that evidence. No fabricated customer proof was added to satisfy the requested score.
