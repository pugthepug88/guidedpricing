# Business Phone — focused calling compositions

28 October 2026. Scope: Power Dialler, Phone quick actions and the supporting numbers/recording area. This supersedes the preceding wide, flat application panels and large paired support cards.

The owner rejected the horizontal after-call panel, asked for a recognisable phone/keypad and useful actions arranged around it, then pointed out the same spread/flatness in Power Dialler and excess size/emptiness in the number/media cards. A generated design reference was displayed in this conversation. The owner responded “yeah! something like that!” and requested a visible customisation/add-your-own cue. That supports the concept direction; it is not acceptance of the final native implementation.

The existing actual wordmark and approved portrait sheet render natively in the page.

Reference prompt: premium wide Zapla Business Phone section; short copy on the left; one upright narrow browser webphone with a restrained silver double edge, white glass, soft shadow and local neutral light; actual Zapla wordmark, Mia and completed call; recognisable compact numeric keypad with no phone number/count/time; blue/coral/ink custom shortcuts Pipeline, Follow-up, Booking; enlarged Add to pipeline / Start follow-up / Book a visit callouts around the lower phone with accurate review-save/workflow/page-opening consequences. Keep one focal phone, legible UI and generous space. Exclude mobile hardware/notch/battery claims, giant dashboards, settings tables, tilted stacks, fake metrics, AI petal, neon and washed-out pastel feature cards.

## Resulting design decisions

- **Power Dialler:** a compact CRM calling queue beside one focal calling dock. Mia's portrait/current call and human-saved outcome have the most weight; Daniel is visibly next. Next → Call now remains a deliberate human sequence. Optional Auto Dial is separate supporting copy. Desktop puts this calling scene left and copy right; normal DOM reading order puts the explanation first when stacked. No keypad is duplicated here.
- **Quick actions:** the approved narrow silver browser-phone direction, with a quiet keypad for recognition, three coloured labelled shortcuts and larger matching callouts connected by short static lines. These enlarge the useful actions rather than creating another flat row. A separate editorial “Add your own shortcut” cue mentions name/icon/colours/action. It is not an invented inline settings control inside the phone. The phone is labelled Browser phone and has no native mobile hardware indicators.
- **Phone breadth:** remove the large outer feature-card shells. Eight purchasing markets remain legible in a compact two-column list with region abbreviations. Recording/transcription become a small history widget with media labels and a shorter prepared excerpt. Availability/local requirements/rates remain visible. AI Receptionist and Unified Inbox links are condensed while retaining their distinct roles.

The whole-page sequence stays photographic warm white → one contained charcoal calling scene → white/silver phone-and-shortcuts scene → compact open support information → neutral setup → established final CTA/sky. Blue/coral/ink belong to the shortcut illustration; no feature colour expands into a page-wide theme. The hero photograph, main proposition, CTA typography/scale, route, draft SEO and unrelated pages remain.

## Capability and interaction boundaries

No new calling capability is claimed. The keypad is noninteractive and hidden from assistive technology; readable product names/actions remain available. Static calling/shortcut shapes are not focusable dead buttons. The website cannot place calls, start a workflow, change contacts, buy a number or book anything. No animation, autoplay, scroll capture or selector is introduced.

Pipeline still means a person reviews/saves the lead entry. Follow-up starts a chosen configured workflow; ending a call does not itself send messages. Book a visit opens a chosen booking page and does not show a confirmed booking. The new transcript wording is prepared dialogue consistent with Mia's warm-white options request, not an edit to a genuine recorded customer conversation. Numbers/usage and Guided Launch boundaries, A2P FAQ and exclusions remain.

## Independent review and response

Product Marketing/Conversion, SME buyer and buyer psychology: independent source review found no material capability blocker, checked the static keypad and external customisation cue, and requested “continue when you're ready” rather than an unproven ability to choose any arbitrary queued contact. The wording is corrected.

Brand/Creative, UI/UX and Motion: independent review inspected the generated reference and source. It identified repeated copy-left/phone-right geometry, callouts that were too small, missing visual mapping, and an Auto Dial selector conflict. The Power desktop composition is reversed, callouts enlarged to 220–240px with 15px titles/13px descriptions, static mapped connectors added, and the paragraph selector corrected. Final independent source geometry review found both 1201px/801px thresholds fit, connectors remain beneath their related surfaces, and stacked content retains DOM reading order. No current browser assessment was performed.

Root assessment keeps one focal current caller per calling illustration, a distinct keypad/shortcut purpose, and reduced scale for supporting functions. At 1200px the quick-action explanation stacks above its scene; at 1100px Power stacks; at 800px phone/callouts and the queue/caller become normal-flow equivalents. The content has no fixed scene height that can clip growing text. The lines are decorative associations; all meaning remains in labels and ordinary reading order without them.
