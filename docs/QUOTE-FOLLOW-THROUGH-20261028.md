28 October 2026. Route: `/quote-follow-up`. Base: `6cd3959`.

Supersedes the cabinetry composition in `QUOTE-FOLLOW-UP-REDESIGN-20261028.md`. The owner reopened the page's composition, content hierarchy and cross-industry positioning. Lovable remains read-only. All source changes go through the existing GitHub repository.

## Independent assessment

The workshop was chosen to make a considered quote decision tangible. Giving it hero scale narrowed the apparent audience to cabinetry/trades. The source record and prepared conversation explained a plausible workflow, but the photograph displaced the central Zapla promise. The previous favourable visual score did not establish that the page was commercially finished.

Follow-up itself is not an established competitive moat: many platforms have reminders. The page should explain Zapla's connected follow-through and the value of the agreed implementation, without claiming exclusivity, customer results or a proven revenue advantage. `/follow-up` remains the broader solution across enquiries, quotes, bookings and repeat business. This revision changes only the quote solution and links back to that umbrella; it does not silently replace the broader page.

## Final page and capability boundaries

- The opening leads with “You send the quote. Zapla follows through.” A compact quote context connects to a prepared SMS two days later and a useful customer question about staging the work.
- The next section retains the same Emma/project/question. Alex deliberately reviews the scope and records “Prepare a staged quote.” The illustration does not imply an automated answer, inferred intent, automatic task completion, quote acceptance or a won job.
- Guided Launch explains the service behind the configured workflow: starting event, wording/channel/timing, reply handling and team work, followed by the required separate setup and usage terms.
- Practical FAQs cover existing quoting tools, timing, replies, buyer fit, older opportunities and setup. The original source inventory and pricing/booking destination remain.
- No photograph or quote register is used in this page. The old asset remains for historical rollback and is not loaded by the new component.

Evidence: the existing 28 October quote reassessment, `AUTOMATION-BUILDER-EVIDENCE.md` including its superseding live-product observations and current UI evidence, and `QUOTES-INVOICES-SIGNATURES-EVIDENCE.md`. Documented/configurable messaging, waits, Inbox and customer notes support the prepared story. Specific start events, connected accounts, reply handling and workflow exits are agreed during setup. No universal stop-on-reply, queued-message cancellation, integration parity, guaranteed response or claimed ROI is introduced. The scene is an explanation, not a screenshot or verified delivery/run.
