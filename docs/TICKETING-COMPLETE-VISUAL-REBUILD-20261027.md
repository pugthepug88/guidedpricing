## Design decision

The former page was an undifferentiated small board, an extended café repair record and a dark text band. The replacement uses four different visual jobs:

- One white customer request in focus, within an ink/steel support list. Three subordinate rows establish a working queue, with people and stage. No toolbar, fake controls, empty lanes or synthetic counters.
- A fresh panoramic photograph of colleagues reviewing work. A compact customer, related pipeline item and assigned-team strip sits below the photograph without covering people.
- One contained silver board with three custom stages, alongside open field/file/tag detail. This demonstrates adaptable processes and useful context without another full application tour.
- One consolidated Guided Launch and native FAQ panel, followed by the established final CTA and root sky/footer.

USE the current Automation hero's restrained silver edging, readable white foreground and shared typography. ADAPT its contained material depth to an ink/steel support surface; do not copy the AI scene's neon, glow, motion or workflow claims. USE the current Chatbot page's distinct composition roles and compact interface detail. REJECT the incumbent beige board, café photograph/pressure story, dark process-list band, oversized original-request record and equal boxed treatment for every idea.

A materially different alternative was a centred headline above a full-width dashboard. Rejected: it would again give a broad interface most of the opening's space, lengthen the first product explanation and repeat Pipeline's board-led identity. The selected request/list has one clear focal point and a different visual language from the lower board.
