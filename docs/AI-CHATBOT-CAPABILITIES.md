## Demonstration boundaries

- The fixed illustrative customer is Mia Thompson, `mia@example.com`. The visitor supplies both before a contact is shown. No identification of anonymous visitors is implied.
- The request is quoted as the customer's original words: “A replacement. We’d like it sorted next week.” Do not imply an AI-generated summary, lead score, budget inference or guaranteed warm lead.
- Qualification can ask relevant questions; budget is optional and context-dependent. The scene does not claim the answers are automatically mapped into specific custom fields.
- Pipeline placement and a task to review the enquiry are an agreed configuration, not a universal default. No automatic assignee, deadline, completed call or completed task is shown.
- Optional booking continues the same illustrative enquiry. Calendar availability is offered and the customer explicitly agrees before the booking result. A captured contact is not automatically an appointment.
- Handover is a separate conditional request. Show its original question and conversation context; do not invent notifications, assignment, AI summaries, automatic pausing or instant staff availability.
- The widget illustration has no input or send control. Native booking disclosure expands explanatory content; it never books. All values are fixed and the demonstration collects or transmits no visitor data.
- Existing help for public Agent Chat describes only current-session display. It does not independently prove universal persisted transcript history. Do not generalise that limitation into a denial of the separately documented contact tools or human escalation with context.
- No unlimited AI, response-time SLA, conversion rate, revenue, customer testimonial, headcount guarantee or universal external-system connection is claimed.
