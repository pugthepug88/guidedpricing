- The request is quoted as the customer's original words: “A replacement. We’d like it sorted next week.” Do not imply an AI-generated summary, lead score, budget inference or guaranteed warm lead.

- The widget illustration has no input or send control. Native booking disclosure expands explanatory content; it never books. All values are fixed and the demonstration collects or transmits no visitor data.
- Existing help for public Agent Chat describes only current-session display. It does not independently prove universal persisted transcript history. Do not generalise that limitation into a denial of the separately documented contact tools or human escalation with context.
- No unlimited AI, response-time SLA, conversion rate, revenue, customer testimonial, headcount guarantee or universal external-system connection is claimed.
