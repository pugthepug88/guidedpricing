# Calendars & Bookings: capability evidence

Checked **23 October 2026 (Australia/Sydney)** for `/features/calendars-bookings`.

This is a documentation review, not live product acceptance testing. No appointment was created, no calendar account was connected, and no sales calendar embed was installed or tested.

## Verified capability matrix

| Capability            | Confirmed behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Boundary for this page                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Availability settings | Calendar and user availability have day switches, start/end times, multiple ranges per day and a timezone. Appointment types have duration and optional minimum notice.                                                            | Show working days, hours and a lunch break. Do not invent buffer periods, daily limits, holiday overrides or cancellation windows. Do not imply calendar hours are the only scheduling input.                                                            |
| Booking links         | Public calendar links can be copied and shared. A slug is optional. Calendars can be activated or deactivated. Embed code is also documented.                                                                                      | Explain a shareable booking page. The Zapla sales embed remains deferred and untouched.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Customer selection    | If multiple services exist, the customer chooses one. They choose an available date and time, provide details, review a summary and complete the booking. Unavailable days are disabled. A single service skips service selection. | Use one coherent appointment example. A marketing selector may change dates, slots and a summary without collecting data or submitting a booking.                                                                                                        |
| Timezone display      | Times default to the customer's detected timezone; a dropdown changes the displayed timezone.                                                                                                                                      | Display a clear timezone. Do not claim universal conflict prevention.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Confirmation          | The public booking flow shows a confirmation and success state after completion.                                                                                                                                                   | **Automatic booking confirmation email or SMS is not independently verified.** Do not turn on-page confirmation into an automatic message-delivery claim. A generic help reference to meeting confirmation emails does not establish this behaviour.     |
| Reminders             | The official Calendar & Bookings feature page explicitly names automated email and SMS reminders.                                                                                                                                  | Treat this as a provider-documented public capability. Detailed reminder setup, defaults, exact timing, per-booking controls and delivery behaviour are not established by the help articles. No specific 24-hour/2-hour sequence or delivery guarantee. |
| Calendar connections  | Current help explicitly documents Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and Apple iCloud. Users select the calendar on a connected account; assigned team members can have a chosen destination for booking events.                       | Name only these providers. Do not imply all calendars, universal two-way behaviour or guaranteed conflict elimination. Older Google connections may need reconnection for event-creation access.                                                         |
| Team scheduling       | Team members can be assigned to a calendar and given booking destinations. Four calendar types are listed: one-to-one, many-to-one, one-to-many and many-to-many. Exact allocation algorithms, round-robin fairness, pooled availability semantics and routing guarantees are not established. Do not invent them.                                                                            |
| Rescheduling          | Staff can open a booking and save a new start and end time.                                                                                                                                                                        | **Customer changes require contacting the calendar owner in the current public booking help.** Do not show customer self-service rescheduling links.                                                                                                     |
| Cancellation          | Staff can update booking status. Confirmed and cancelled are default statuses; custom statuses are supported.                                                                                                                      | Customers contact the business to cancel. | Team booking view     | Bookings support list and calendar views, month/week/day grids, search, calendar filters and a detail panel with contact, calendar, time and notes.                                                                                | Show a purposeful appointment record rather than invented dashboard totals or activity feeds.                                                                                                                                                            |

Omit payment-at-booking claims. Do not promise elimination of double bookings, scheduling conflicts or no-shows.                                                                                    |

## Role of existing project evidence

- `docs/FEATURE-PAGES-BRIEF.md`, `docs/ZAPLA-WEBSITE-BUILD-PROTOCOL.md` and `docs/ZAPLA-WEBSITE-DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md` govern architecture, brand, demonstration integrity and review. They are not a live scheduling product test.
- `src/routes/booking.tsx` and `docs/BOOKING-PAGE-DRAFT.md` establish that `/booking` is Zapla's sales-call destination and its actual calendar embed is pending. This feature page must neither replace that route nor simulate its appointment submission.
- `src/components/features/AIChatbotPage.tsx`, `src/routes/ai-receptionist.tsx`, `src/routes/crm.tsx` and the booking examples in `src/routes/industries/` and `src/components/industries/` establish site context and adjacent-page ownership. Illustrative booking exchanges do not independently certify calendar integrations, confirmation delivery or change rules.
- Unified Inbox and Pipeline Management establish Zapla's visual language. Their section arrangements are not a required template, and an unfinished draft is not an approved reference merely because it is present on main.

## Marketing demonstration contract

The demonstration is explicitly labelled, uses real date/time conventions, and only changes local illustration state. It does not accept personal information, create appointments, send messages or provide a fake booking-submission button. Selection changes explain the customer's experience; separate supporting content explains the team's control and management. Staff changes and customer requests remain distinct. All sales CTAs point to `/booking`.

Rendered website QA and build checks are recorded separately from these product evidence limits. Successful marketing-page interactions must not be described as verified live calendar behaviour.
