# AI Chatbot visual reset, 26 October 2026

## Authority and design decision

The owner rejected the compact-panel revision as wordy, requested a full reset against Birdeye, and approved the proposed rebuild. This supersedes the transcript/contact hero, interactive service-policy document and separate setup/FAQ layout. The H1, final CTA, route/indexing, shared navigation/footer and /booking remain.

The primary promise is answering website visitors, capturing useful enquiries and arranging suitable appointments while the team is busy. The page must establish business context visually, then use short exchanges and concrete outcomes. More text inside a smaller card did not solve the previous problem.

Reference decisions: USE Birdeye's recognisable business websites and compact widget proportions; ADAPT varied photography/product compositions and short benefit-led explanations; REJECT copied imagery, navigation, customer proof, metrics, unsupported reporting, assignment, AI summaries and chat-to-text claims. Existing Zapla type, palette, portrait sheet and petal remain.
