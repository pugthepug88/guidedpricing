# Feature pages: approved direction, 21 October 2026

## Architecture
Platform remains Overview, CRM, Customer Marketing, AI Receptionist. Features is a separate dropdown linking directly to individual pages. Category headings are menu organisation, not extra landing pages. Add only completed destinations to navigation.

Initial page: /features/unified-inbox. AI Chatbot belongs beside Unified Inbox as a separate future feature page; the two are not interchangeable. AI Assistant scope must be clarified before creating another overlapping item.

Future grouping: Leads & conversations (AI Chatbot, Unified Inbox); Sales & automation (Pipeline Management, Automation Builder, Calendars & Bookings, Quotes/Invoices/Signatures); Marketing & operations (Websites/Funnels/Forms, Social Planner, Ticketing, Reporting & Analytics). Meeting Rooms, Whiteboard and apps are supporting capabilities initially.

## Visual direction
ADAPT Birdeye's contained textured blue stage and layered frosted panel depth, not its mascot, copy or product claims. ADAPT ActiveCampaign's editorial photography and varied open/enclosed section rhythm. USE existing Zapla colours, Manrope, portrait sheet and root-owned header/footer. REJECT repeated coloured heading fragments, tiny decorative dashboard controls, arbitrary metrics and all-card layouts.

Eight sections in this first page: hero and selectable inbox; compact customer problems; interactive conversation/context/team views; editorial human section; connection to CRM/follow-up; Guided Launch; FAQs; booking CTA blending into shared footer. Simple capabilities may need fewer sections.

## Scope and evidence
Current src/routes/crm.tsx contains ConversationSurface (SMS/email conversations alongside customer profile), customer fields/tags/owners/notes/pipeline stages, shared team context, unlimited users and Guided Launch. src/routes/platform.tsx corroborates the shared customer-record proposition. These existing approved site claims are the source of this draft; they are not a new hands-on product audit. Do not expand to other channels, inbox collision detection, AI summaries, automatic routing or integration guarantees without product evidence.

Conversation names and exchanges are explanatory marketing illustrations, not testimonials or live data. No performance numbers, outcomes or customer endorsements are claimed. No dummy send controls; selectors change the illustrated conversation only.

## Implementation constraints
Keep global footer unchanged and calendar embed last. Booking CTAs /booking. New route has noindex and remains outside SEARCH_PAGES. No staticData.sitemap false on this route, since that hides it from Lovable's page selector. Generated TanStack route tree is generated by build, not manually maintained. New styles are page-scoped. No production publish authorised in this task.
