## Story, colour and motion decisions

Six parts: browser-calling promise and one current call; major Power Dialler selected-list/current-contact/human-note/next-contact scene; fresh owner photograph with notes and human follow-through; incoming access and conditional recording/history breadth plus related-feature links; one Guided Launch/FAQ chapter; established final invitation and shared sky/footer.

The canvas is warm white. Silver contains the opening phone; one inset ink chapter gives Power Dialler priority; photography then open neutral content releases that density. Lower practical content is cool neutral, followed by the usual warm-white CTA/sky. No feature accent becomes a whole-page colour theme, no successive beige bands, coloured-icon pastel tiles, tilted stacks, decorative numbered steps, fake metrics or ornamental accent bars.

Static is the deliberate Motion Director recommendation: human action and optional product automation would otherwise be easy to confuse. No scene playback, selector, focus movement, scroll capture, sticky trap or hidden story is introduced. Normal flow, reduced motion, mobile and no-JavaScript all retain the complete page; native FAQ disclosure is the only page-owned interactive content. The shared footer retains its existing behaviour.
