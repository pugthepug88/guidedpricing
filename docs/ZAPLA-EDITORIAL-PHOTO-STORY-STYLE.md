# Zapla editorial photo story style

This is the name for the mortgage broker page's approved hero direction. It extends Zapla's incumbent system, with the current Dental glass treatment.

## What creates the look

1. Oversized, tightly spaced Inter Tight headings, Manrope body copy, warm white space and muted sage emphasis.
2. One dominant candid human photograph, with a large asymmetric curve on the hero's upper left corner.
3. Compact translucent cards sitting across the photograph's lower edges. They show meaningful client events, with one causal story per scene.
4. A warm white glass surface at 91% opacity, 2px light border, thin inset edge, 6px blur, soft shadow and 18px radius.
5. Restrained natural editorial photography with cream, oak, sage and ink accents. No posed handshake, artificial UI inside images or generic AI symbols.
6. Whole-page rhythm: open typography, a smaller photo story, a broad photographic feature, open software fit and a dark implementation section. Photo/card scale changes with the job each scene does.
7. Mobile composition is designed separately. Cards remain readable, with supporting overlays placed below the photo when needed. Motion advances once and holds the result, with pause, replay and reduced-motion completion.

## Reusable prompt

> Design this page in Zapla’s editorial photo story style, matching the approved Mortgage Broker hero and the latest Dental glass treatment. Use oversized tightly spaced typography, generous warm white space, muted sage accents and a dominant candid human photograph with an asymmetric curved corner. Place compact translucent cards across the lower photo edges. Give them thin light borders, a subtle inset edge, soft shadows and restrained blur. Each card must show a meaningful client event, and each scene must tell one clear cause-and-effect story. Generate fresh natural photography with space reserved for overlays and faces kept clear. Vary image scale and section composition across the page, including one broad photographic feature. Inspect the full desktop and mobile pages, keep cards readable, and use brief one-way motion with a final hold and reduced-motion support. Avoid decorative dashboard clutter, invented metrics and identical repeated layouts.

The structural reference is a starting point. Adapt the client story to the industry; do not copy dental workflows into a broker page.
