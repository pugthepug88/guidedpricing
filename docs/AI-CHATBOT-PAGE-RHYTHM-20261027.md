Changing the photograph does not change the reading task. Playback asks for a click and time without providing authentic product footage. ## Awareness and conversion

| Visitor state | Question answered | Page treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unaware | What am I looking at, and why would it matter? | A recognisable business website/widget and a team occupied with paid work. |
| Problem aware | What happens while we are busy or closed? | Opening copy makes answering, contact capture and after-hours coverage explicit. |
| Solution aware | Can a chatbot produce something useful for our team? | Original enquiry, name/email, chosen pipeline and configured acknowledgement together. |
| Product aware | Can Zapla book and follow our rules? | Explicit offer/agreement/appointment, then supplied booking rules and a matching answer. |
| Most aware | What will setup involve, what does it cost and what do I do next? | Guided Launch, practical FAQs, pricing link and /booking action. |

These are overlapping entry points, not five extra sections or labels shown to buyers. Native FAQ disclosure keeps detailed objections available without expanding the main sales narrative.
